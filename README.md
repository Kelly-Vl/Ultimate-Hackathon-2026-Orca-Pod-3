# Ultimate Hackathon: Vibe to Survive

Presented by Coders for Causes, the UWA AI Club and the UWA Data Science Club, this beginner-friendly hackathon will challenge teams to develop creative solutions to problems arising in a post-apocalyptic world.

Throughout the day, participants will also complete challenges designed to help them strengthen and improve their solutions before presenting their final concepts to the judging panel.

With no technical experience required, the Ultimate Hackathon offers a fun and supportive environment for participants to build practical problem-solving and teamwork skills. Anyone with a passion for developing ideas, collaborating with others and tackling unusual challenges is encouraged to take part.

## Matilda Bay 

Matilda Bay became the refuge for a group of orca pods. The Giant Peacock's feathers tore through the western side of campus. They scattered storage facilities and destroyed most of the paths and vehicles once used to move supplies.
Between them, the orcas hold enough water, food and medicine. The shortage is not in the total amount. It is in the distribution. Stock sits divided between pods, transport is slow and damaged, and smaller peacocks keep stealing cargo and blocking routes.

### Orca Pod 3

Overproduces food through a surviving greenhouse setup, but stopped sharing after a trade convoy was intercepted. The pod now hoards out of distrust, not genuine need.

### Data Given

1. Pod inventory & needs
Stock and consumption levels for water, food, and medicine across all four pods, including status flags and delivery records.

2. Consumption trends
Daily resource levels for each pod over the reporting period, showing day-to-day consumption changes.

3. Council allocations
Records from six bay-wide allocation meetings showing each pod's requests, needs, and resource distribution outcomes.
